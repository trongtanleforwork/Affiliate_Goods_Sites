At **OkeyReview Goods**, we are committed to delivering authentic, transparent, and trustworthy consumer product reviews. This page explains our affiliate partnerships in accordance with the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) requirements.

## FTC Affiliate Disclosure

In compliance with FTC guidelines (16 CFR Part 255), please be aware that links on [https://goods.okeyreview.com/](https://goods.okeyreview.com/) may be affiliate links. If you click on a product link and make a purchase (across Amazon Associates, ShareASale, CJ Affiliate, or direct brand stores), OkeyReview may receive a commission.

**This does not increase the price you pay.** You pay the exact same price (or discounted price if we share a promotional coupon).

## How We Evaluate Physical Products

Our testing and evaluation process remains 100% independent:
* **Objective Analysis:** We assess product durability, materials, ingredient safety, ergonomics, and real-world utility.
* **Unbiased Recommendations:** Commercial relationships never dictate our pros, cons, or final buying recommendations.
