Welcome to **OkeyReview Goods** ([https://goods.okeyreview.com/](https://goods.okeyreview.com/)). We value your privacy and are committed to safeguarding personal information collected on our platform.

## 1. Information Collected Automatically

When browsing our buying guides, our servers collect standard non-identifiable technical data:
* IP address and geographic region.
* Browser version, operating system, and device type.
* Referring URLs and time spent on specific review pages.

## 2. Cookies & Affiliate Tracking

* **Affiliate Cookies:** When clicking merchant links (such as Amazon, ShareASale, CJ, or direct merchant stores), affiliate cookies are placed on your device to attribute qualifying purchases.
* **Cookie Control:** You can modify your browser settings to decline cookies or notify you when cookies are placed.

## 3. Use of Information

We use collected technical information solely to:
* Improve website speed, navigation, and user experience.
* Identify which product categories (beauty, home living, auto gear) are most helpful to our readers.
* Respond to user inquiries and product submission requests.

## 4. Third-Party Retailers

Our reviews link directly to third-party e-commerce stores. OkeyReview Goods does not operate these stores and is not responsible for their privacy standards or data collection practices.
